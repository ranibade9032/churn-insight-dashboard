01 / Executive Overview

From Customer Data to Retention Intelligence

A structured view of churn patterns, customer risk and business opportunities.

EDA-ONLY PROJECT

CHURN INSIGHT investigates historical customer accounts to understand where churn is concentrated, which customer and service characteristics are associated with attrition, and how customer departures affect monthly recurring revenue.

Business question: Where should retention teams focus their resources to improve customer continuity and protect recurring revenue?
Project Objective

Audit and clean the dataset, establish baseline metrics, examine lifecycle and commercial patterns, assess service relationships, quantify revenue exposure and develop exactly three prioritized retention recommendations.

Technology & Methodology

Python 3 · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn

09 / Executive Action Plan

Three Prioritized Retention Strategies

Proposed initiatives and projected ARR impacts stated in the notebook.

PRIORITY 01 · IMMEDIATE

First-Year Anchor Program

Business problem: Early-life churn and high attrition among month-to-month subscribers.

Target: New customers in their first 12 months, especially those on month-to-month contracts.

Intervention: Day 7, Day 30 and Day 60 onboarding check-ins, followed by a Month-6 contract migration offer.

Success metric: 90-day retention and 12-month cohort churn.

$110,000
Projected ARR impact · notebook estimate
PRIORITY 02 · SERVICE VALUE

Fiber Care & Security Bundle

Business problem: Fiber Optic subscribers have elevated observed churn.

Target: Fiber customers, particularly those reporting service or support friction.

Intervention: Pilot a security and premium tech-support bundle with proactive service-quality monitoring.

Success metric: Fiber churn, support attachment and service-quality outcomes.

$162,000
Projected ARR impact · notebook estimate
PRIORITY 03 · CUSTOMER CARE

Senior Care & Auto-Pay Desk

Business problem: Senior customer groups and selected payment cohorts show higher observed churn.

Target: Senior customers and eligible bank-withdrawal or mailed-check users.

Intervention: Dedicated human support and a controlled auto-pay migration incentive test.

Success metric: Retention, payment migration and customer satisfaction.

$66,000
Projected ARR impact · notebook estimate

11 / Analytical Governance

Limitations & Interpretation

Responsible use of historical customer data.

  • This project uses retrospective exploratory data analysis. Predictive modeling and automated classifiers are excluded.
  • Observed associations and percentage-point differences do not establish causality.
  • Longer contracts and service add-ons may be associated with other customer characteristics, including pre-existing loyalty.
  • Annualized revenue exposure is an illustrative run-rate calculation, not a guaranteed future loss.
  • Projected ARR impacts are estimates for proposed interventions, not confirmed business results.
  • Final decisions should use controlled pilots and independently verified notebook outputs.
Executive principle: Use these findings to prioritize investigations and pilot programs. Do not interpret observational patterns as proof that any single factor causes customer churn.
