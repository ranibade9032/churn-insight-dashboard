Strategic Recommendations

Three prioritized, evidence-backed initiatives

01

First-Year Anchor Program

Introduce a structured 90-day onboarding journey, customer check-ins and carefully tested contract migration offers.

Target: New customers and month-to-month subscribers.
02

Fiber Care & Security Bundle

Investigate fiber service issues and test security and premium tech-support bundles for at-risk customers.

Target: Fiber Optic customers.
03

Senior Care & Auto-Pay Support

Offer proactive assistance to senior customers and simplify payment-method changes; evaluate impact through a pilot.

Target: Senior customers and high-friction payment groups.
