# CHURN INSIGHT — Customer Retention Intelligence

### A Comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis & Strategic Decision-Support Report

## 📌 Project Overview

**CHURN INSIGHT** is a customer analytics project designed to explore customer churn patterns, identify potential retention risks, analyze revenue exposure, and support data-driven business decisions.

The project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and interactive visualizations to understand customer behavior and highlight areas where customer retention strategies can be improved.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer churn and retention patterns.
- Identify customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Explore the relationship between contracts, services, and customer churn.
- Understand the potential recurring revenue at risk.
- Recommend data-driven customer retention strategies.
- Present key findings through a professional dashboard.

## 📊 Key Features

- **Customer Overview:** Total customers, retained customers, and churn rate.
- **Churn Analysis:** Visual comparison of retained and churned customers.
- **Contract Analysis:** Compare churn rates across contract types.
- **Service Analysis:** Explore churn patterns associated with additional services.
- **Revenue Insights:** Examine recurring revenue and estimated revenue exposure.
- **Strategic Recommendations:** Identify practical opportunities to improve retention.
- **Professional Dashboard:** Modern dark theme with blue, cyan, purple, and green accents.

## 📈 Project Insights

The analysis explores important customer retention indicators, including:

- Customer churn rate and retention rate.
- Churn patterns across different contract types.
- Early-tenure customer retention risks.
- Associations between optional services and churn.
- Potential recurring revenue exposure.

These findings can help businesses identify areas for further investigation and develop targeted retention initiatives.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Onboarding:** Provide additional guidance and support to new customers.
2. **Strengthen Retention:** Investigate why short-term contract customers are more likely to leave.
3. **Enhance Service Experience:** Review support quality and customer service issues.
4. **Monitor High-Risk Segments:** Track churn indicators and customer feedback.
5. **Measure Results:** Test retention initiatives and evaluate their effectiveness using data.
